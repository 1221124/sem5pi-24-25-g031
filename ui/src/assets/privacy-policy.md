# Privacy Policy for SARM G031 System

**Effective Date:** 2024/12/17

---

## Introduction

The privacy and security of your personal data are of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal data we collect, how it is used, stored, and retained, and your rights concerning this data. By using the SARM G031 system, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users, including patients, administrators, and other individuals who interact with the system. Please read the following information carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data and how we will treat it.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

We collect and process personal data in the course of providing our services. This data may include:

- **Personal Identification Information**: Name, email address, contact details, date of birth, gender, etc.  
- **Medical Data**: Health records, treatments, diagnoses, and other medical information related to your healthcare.  
- **Login Data**: Username, password, authentication tokens, and other information required to verify your identity.  
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with the system, including IP addresses, browser type, device information, and usage patterns.  

---

## 2. Purpose of Data Collection

We collect and process personal data for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain healthcare services.  
- To manage user accounts and authentication.  
- To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.  
- To ensure the security of our systems and prevent fraud.  
- To improve the functionality and performance of the system.  
- To communicate with you about updates, notifications, and important information regarding your healthcare.  

---

## 3. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including compliance with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations. The following outlines our data retention practices:

- **Medical Records**: Your medical records will be retained forever, in compliance with applicable healthcare regulations.
- **Personal Data**: Personal data will be retained forever or until your account is deleted, whichever comes first.  
- **Anonymized Data**: Some anonymized data may be retained for research, analysis, and statistical purposes.  
- **Deletion Requests**: You can request deletion of your data via your profile settings.  

---

## 4. Data Deletion Procedures

- **Patient-Initiated Deletion**: You can request deletion through your profile settings. Upon verification via a link sent to your email, all data will be permanently deleted.  
- **Admin-Initiated Deletion**: Administrators can delete accounts, resulting in immediate and permanent data erasure.  

> *Note: Certain data may be retained for legal or contractual purposes.*  

---

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

- **Consent**: When you provide explicit consent during registration or specific requests.  
- **Contractual Necessity**: To perform services under a contract.  
- **Legal Obligation**: To comply with legal or regulatory requirements.  
- **Legitimate Interests**: For business interests such as improving services, ensuring security, and preventing fraud.  

---

## 6. Sharing Your Data

We may share your personal data in the following circumstances:

- **With Service Providers**: Trusted third parties assisting us in providing services (e.g., hosting or email services).  
- **With Legal Authorities**: When required by law or legal process.  
- **For Research Purposes**: Anonymized data may be used for research or statistical analysis.  

---

## 7. Security of Your Data

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement measures such as:

- Secure encryption of sensitive data (e.g., passwords - Auth0 (IAM) responsability - and medical records).
- Access controls for authorized personnel only.

> *However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.*

---

## 8. Your Rights Regarding Your Personal Data

As a user, you have the following rights:

- **Right to Access**: Request access to the personal data we hold.  
- **Right to Rectification**: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.  
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your data (*Right to be Forgotten*).  
- **Right to Object**: Object to the processing of your data in certain cases.  
- **Right to Data Portability**: Receive your data in a transferable format.  

---
